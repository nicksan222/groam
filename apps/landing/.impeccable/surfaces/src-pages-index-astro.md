## Direction contract

THESIS: The trip is a big printed tour bill; an idea is a yellow strip pasted over it, and it only sticks when someone applies it. Refuses the white SaaS page with a product card beside a headline.

OWN-WORLD: Drenched cobalt bill fields, cool poster paper, ink black, paste-strip yellow for ideas and changes, sky for agents, four flat poster inks for travelers. One family, Archivo: condensed heavy uppercase display set edge to edge, normal-width text, tabular timetable figures. Square corners, 3px ink rules. Proposed strips sit lifted and tilted with a dashed paste edge; applied strips lie flush. Raises: state is drawn, not coloured (Jackfield); figures change by a physical strip swap, never a fade (Nixie); one dominant object owns each viewport and copy rides on it (Polish poster).

STORY: The visitor sees the plan as one bill, watches an idea get reviewed and pasted down, believes nothing changes behind their back, and starts a trip.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Cobalt bill fills the screen. Nav printed along its top edge. GET THE TRIP OUT OF THE GROUP CHAT. at display scale across the left two thirds; one-line sub; Start a trip as a solid paper block, See how it works as a ruled link. Right third: the printed Lisbon Saturday timetable; Maya's Sintra strip slides over the Belém rows, approvals tick on, then it presses flat as Applied. Pause control and Example trip label on the bill.

FORM: Tour bill with paste-over strips, position 7 on the ordered list, seed key db11c2ba. Code-led. Signature interaction: the strip paste (slide in lifted, review, press flush); finished still under reduced motion.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
